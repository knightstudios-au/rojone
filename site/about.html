Our story

Forty-seven years of Australian manufacturing

Rojone began in 1979 with Eleonore Cato assembling cables at home in Sydney. Today it designs, manufactures and distributes RF, microwave and fibre optic components from a purpose-built facility in Ingleburn, NSW, for defence, telecommunications, transport and industrial customers across Australia.
