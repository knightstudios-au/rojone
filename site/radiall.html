Radiall

Radiall designs and manufactures high-performance interconnect solutions, including RF connectors, cable assemblies, fibre optic components and multipin connectors. Founded in 1952, the company serves demanding aerospace, defence, telecommunications and industrial applications. Rojone has distributed Radiall in Australia since 1984 and can help you select the right parts for your project.
